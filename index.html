Abhilash Prakash's Lionel Messi Shots and Goals Under Different Managers makes shot locations immediately recognizable through repeated pitches. It also creates four concrete comparison problems that the redesign addresses.

01

Overlap hides concentration

Hundreds of opaque circles compete in central areas.

Density bins reveal the shape; shot mode preserves the events.
02

Totals hide exposure

Longer manager periods naturally create more marks.

Per-90 rates use Messi's audited on-pitch minutes.
03

Comparison relies on memory

Eight pitches split across two images are hard to compare.

One linked timeline keeps the A/B views aligned.
04

Sources change mid-series

The original combines providers and measurement systems.

One pinned StatsBomb snapshot makes the contract explicit.
METHOD NOTES

What the evidence can support

Full methodology →
01

Scope

Barcelona La Liga, 2004/05–2020/21. The default excludes penalties; direct free kicks remain filterable inside the non-penalty total.

02

Measurement

Playing time is the union of lineup intervals, capped at 90 regulation minutes per match. Stoppage-time shots remain in the numerator.

03

Interpretation

Differences are descriptive. Age, role, teammates, opponents and historical coding also vary across the recorded periods.
